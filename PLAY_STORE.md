# Android / Google Play checklist

1. Wrap the web UI using Capacitor or a native Android shell.
2. Implement Google Play Billing subscription products for Free, Creator ($10), Studio ($50), and Agency ($100) monthly plans where permitted and supported.
3. Verify purchases and subscription status on a server; never trust the client alone.
4. Enforce usage limits, account authentication, access tiers and cancellation/renewal status server-side.
5. Implement privacy policy, account/data deletion, video-upload disclosure, storage retention, and security controls.
6. Test subscription flows with Play Console test tracks, then build/sign an Android App Bundle (AAB).
7. Prepare store icon, screenshots, listing, content rating and app access details.

This project does not include a compiled APK/AAB, live billing, a production server, or a verified Play Store listing.
